Water Box — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Water Box is an Android hydration logging and visualization app by Hydrate Lab. This policy explains what data the app and its backend services process when you use it.

What data we process

  • Stored only on your device: hydration records, daily goals, selected scenes, studio settings, scene text, and app preferences. Hydration records are never uploaded.
  • If you sign in with Google: Water Box receives your Google account identifier, name, email address, and avatar. A sign-in session and your membership status are stored on Hydrate Lab's backend server (a Cloudflare Worker with D1 storage).
  • Membership purchases: purchase and subscription status is processed to restore purchases and keep membership entitlements in sync.

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In provides account authentication.
  • Google Play Billing processes all subscription payments. Water Box never sees or stores complete card details.
  • RevenueCat reads offering, purchase, restore, and subscription verification events.
  • Cloudflare (Worker + D1) hosts the membership API that stores sign-in sessions and rebuildable membership snapshots.

There are no ads, no behavioral analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs in Water Box.

Health note

Water Box is a hydration logging and visualization tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or personalized medical advice. Consult a qualified professional for advice based on your circumstances.

Deletion and controls

  • Local data: hydration records and all other local data can be removed at any time via Android system settings (Settings → Apps → Water Box → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.
  • Google Play subscription: manage or cancel it in Google Play subscription settings; uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Server account and sign-in data: you can request deletion of your server-side account (session, membership snapshot, and the Google account identifier, name, email, and avatar associated with it) at any time — see Account Deletion.
