Water Box — Account Deletion

How to request deletion

To delete your Water Box server account (sign-in session, membership snapshot, and the Google profile data associated with it: account identifier, name, email, and avatar), send an email from the Google account you signed in with:

mhgd3250905@gmail.com

Please use the subject line “Water Box Account Deletion Request” so the request is routed correctly. Deletion requests are processed manually, usually within 7 days.

What deletion covers

  • Server-side sign-in session and membership snapshot stored in Hydrate Lab's backend (Cloudflare Worker + D1).
  • The Google account identifier, name, email address, and avatar associated with your Water Box account.

What deletion does not cover

  • Data on your device (hydration records, goals, scene settings): remove it yourself via Android Settings → Apps → Water Box → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Google Play subscriptions: cancel them in Google Play subscription settings. Deleting your Water Box account does not cancel an active subscription.
  • Google account data held by Google itself, or purchase records retained by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies.

After deletion

Once the server account is deleted, signing in again creates a fresh account with no previous membership linkage except what Google Play restore can rebuild for active purchases.
